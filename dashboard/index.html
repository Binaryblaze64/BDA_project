Where labels are available

Rules applied

  • Keep product codes as text and choose duplicates deterministically.
  • Reject rows with observed gram nutrients outside 0–100 g/100 g or energy outside 0–900 kcal/100 g.
  • Keep missing nutrients in general analysis. Classification requires at least 3 of 7 observed inputs.
  • Use complete cases for the clustering sample.
